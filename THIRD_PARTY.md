|---|---|---|
| `print-jobs/TONER-1_print-job.ps` | `stories260K` converted to int8, plus its 512-token vocabulary (`tok512.bin`) | MIT (llama2.c / tinyllamas). The model was trained on TinyStories (CDLA-Sharing-1.0) |
| `print-jobs/TONER-1-V_print-job.ps` | The same `stories260K` weights and vocabulary, plus the TONER-1-V doodle weights | MIT for `stories260K`, as above. CC BY 4.0 for the TONER-1-V weights, with attribution to Google's Quick, Draw! dataset |
| TONER-1 Pro (built by `tools/build.sh`, not in this repo) | `stories15M` converted to int8, plus the 32,000-token vocabulary from llama2.c's `tokenizer.bin` | MIT (llama2.c / tinyllamas). ## llama2.c and tinyllamas (MIT)

TONER-1 is Andrej Karpathy's `stories260K` model from [tinyllamas](https://huggingface.co/karpathy/tinyllamas). TONER-1 Pro is his `stories15M`. The forward pass in `engine/engine2.ps` follows [llama2.c](https://github.com/karpathy/llama2.c). `pixelart/src/vendor/model.py` is llama2.c's `model.py`, copied without changes, and the TONER-1-V model and its training use it. Karpathy's checkpoints are not committed (the TONER-1-V checkpoints in `pixelart/ckpt/` are). `tools/fetch_checkpoints.sh` downloads them from Karpathy's public pages and checks their SHA-256.

## TinyStories (CDLA-Sharing-1.0)

`stories260K` and `stories15M` were trained on [TinyStories](https://huggingface.co/datasets/roneneldan/TinyStories). That dataset is by Ronen Eldan and Yuanzhi Li ("TinyStories: How Small Can Language Models Be and Still Speak Coherent English?", 2023, [arXiv:2305.07759](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.07759)) and is licensed under the [Community Data License Agreement, Sharing, Version 1.0](https://cdla.dev/sharing-1-0/). This repo contains no TinyStories text. The stories in `docs/` were generated by the models.

## Quick, Draw! (CC BY 4.0)

The TONER-1-V doodle model, and the judge CNN used to score it, were trained on 171,000 drawings from Google's [Quick, Draw! dataset](https://github.com/googlecreativelab/quickdraw-dataset). That dataset is made available by Google, Inc. under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The changes made were: Google's 28x28 `numpy_bitmap` renderings of 18 categories were rasterized to 16x16 one-bit images and tokenized as 2x2 patches. The drawings themselves are not committed. `pixelart/src/fetch_data.py` downloads them.

The TONER-1-V weights are released under **CC BY 4.0**, with attribution to Google's Quick, Draw! dataset. They are in `engine/doodle_weights.ps`, `pixelart/ckpt/d48L4_16k.pt` and inside `print-jobs/TONER-1-V_print-job.ps`. The same terms apply to the judge CNN in `pixelart/ckpt/judge_cnn.pt`. Suggested attribution:

> TONER-1-V by Jacob Sussman, trained on the Quick, Draw! dataset by Google (CC BY 4.0), rasterized to 16x16 one-bit.
